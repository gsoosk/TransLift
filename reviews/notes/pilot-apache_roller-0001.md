# pilot-apache_roller-0001 — systematic problems

- **Reads before the lazy begin are included in `programmatic-region` boundaries.** The extractor marks the whole `programmatic-region` method as the unit, but per `reviews/apache_roller/calibration.md` the Roller JPA transaction really starts at the first write/`getNamedUpdate`/pessimistic find (`JPAPersistenceStrategy.getEntityManager(true)`, `JPAPersistenceStrategy.java:244-246`), not at method entry. Plain reads via `getEntityManager(false)` before that point auto-commit outside the unit. Seen in `250ee4ce4855` `FrontpageSettings.applyInitial`: ops 2 (`JPAWeblogManagerImpl.java:409`, weblog lookup), 3 (`JPAPersistenceStrategy.java:223`, roller_properties lookup) and 5 (cache-hit `strategy.load`) all run before the pessimistic find in `JPAPropertiesManagerImpl.compareAndSetProperty` (`JPAPersistenceStrategy.java:154-155`) that actually opens the transaction. Likely cause: the extractor anchors the boundary at method entry/exit for `programmatic-region`, without checking which call first triggers `getEntityManager(true)`.
- **Operations after the commit point (`flush()`) are still counted inside the region**, confirming the pattern already flagged in calibration for `JPAMediaFileManagerImpl`: `1072bc347f3d` `updateThumbnail`'s op 2 (`strategy.refresh`, `JPAMediaFileManagerImpl.java:287`) runs after `roller.flush()` (line 284). One of the six listed sites; worth a general fix rather than per-transaction correction, since any call after the method's `flush()`/commit call should be excluded from the region's operations.
